Industrial design · 3D-printable ESP32 companion

Four ways to give the friend a body.

Real, dimensional product forms — not flat mascots. Each direction carries the SoulMate identity (ivory shell, ribbed sage status-ears, glowing light-ring face) but commits to a different silhouette, construction logic, and stance. Every one is drawn with the seams, grilles, ports, bevels and feet a modeller needs to take it into CAD and onto a print bed. This file is fully self-contained — all art is inline SVG, with no external images or assets.

How to read each direction

Four views (front · 3/4 · side · back), approximate dimensions, full hardware placement (display, speaker, microphone, USB-C, buttons), removable-panel & assembly logic, and 3D-printing feasibility notes. The ribbed ears are functional status lights in every direction, never decoration.
