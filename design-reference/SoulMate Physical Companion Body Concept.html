A soft, ivory, rabbit-eared companion body for SoulMate — where the ears are not decoration but a quiet, glanceable status light, and every state is echoed on the face screen and in the app. Designed to be 3D-printable, ESP32-ready, and emotionally safe.

01

Design principles

The body extends the SoulMate app into the physical world. It should feel like the same product — calm, soft, and trustworthy — and never like a gadget, a toy, or a clinical device.

02

The ears are the status light

Two soft, LED-lit companion antennas communicate what the system is doing during a voice exchange — the physical twin of the app's status pipeline (Listening → Transcribing → Preparing → Speaking). Crucially, colour is never the only signal: each state pairs a distinct light motion, a face‑screen expression, and matching web‑UI status text.

Accessibility — never colour alone.

Every state is distinguishable by motion pattern (breathing, pulsing, travelling, steady, rhythmic, off) and by the face icon, so the device remains legible for colour‑blind users and at a glance from across a room. The web app always shows a plain‑language status line as the authoritative source of truth.

04

Recommendation

One concept to take forward into prototyping — judged on emotional comfort, HCI clarity, ESP32 realism, print feasibility, and consistency with the SoulMate app.
